# Administrative observation policy

Everything in this release is fictional. Its observation rule is an explicit addition to the teaching world, not an inference that nonresponse is ignorable or that a real institution can access records without consent.

The observation unit is an invited entrant identified by the existing participant files. Cohort membership and baseline response are taken directly from those files. A baseline-survey respondent's recorded exit_month, exit_type and known withdrawal outcome are copied from the original observed registry, as text. The anchor offer comes from the original baseline file; nobody in cohort 2 received an offer. Consent withdrawal leaves the canonical withdrawal_label blank, even though the old registry's withdrawn_12 column stores zero. No original cell or file is edited.

A nonrespondent never entered the frozen mentoring randomization or uptake process. The derived administrative policy records the no-offer/no-uptake realized withdrawal or transfer event through twelve months. It assumes administrative observation without survey participation; no new consent time is drawn. Select the earliest of the stored no-uptake withdrawal time, transfer time and the twelve-month horizon. Resolve an exact tie in the order withdrawal, transfer, horizon, matching the frozen generator's event ordering. Transfer is a competing event: a prior transfer gives withdrawal_label=0 for withdrawal before transfer, not an uncensored twelve-month survival claim.

Only T_withdraw_0 and T_transfer are selected from the author-side frozen population records. These stored values are rounded to six decimals in v1.0.0. Treat them as exact decimals for this release; display a newly derived exit time to three decimal places with half-even rounding. Preserve original respondent time spellings. The frozen nonrespondent inputs contain no exact event/horizon or withdrawal/transfer ties, but the boundary tests specify those cases. This construction does not claim to recover unrounded simulator times.

No grade or support-visit observation is added. No unobserved survey item or latent score is supplied. No outcome is imputed for a consent-censored respondent. The administrative access assumption and the no-offer policy are declared in provenance, not learned from which strategy subsequently scores best.

The permitted ordinary route reads these observed CSVs and the original observed frame/survey files. The author-only code and the hidden files are outside that route. Keeping files under hidden/ is a route distinction, not an access-control guarantee. Later missingness analyses must disclose this new observation rule, keep prediction-time features separate from outcomes, and limit their claims to the declared fictional population and policy.
